About the Research

Sustainable Agriculture
Meets Smart Technology

ALHYDRA is a research initiative that integrates three cultivation paradigms — microalgae bioreactors, vertical horticulture towers, and hydroponic systems — into a single unified structure powered entirely by renewable energy.

An IoT sensor network continuously monitors water quality, environmental conditions, and energy balance. All data is transmitted to Firebase and visualized in a real-time web dashboard accessible from anywhere.

System Architecture

From Field
To Dashboard

1

Sense

ESP32/Arduino reads data from 8 sensors every 30 seconds — pH, light, turbidity, temperature, humidity, water temp, and dual current sensors.

2

Transmit

Sensor readings are sent to Google Firebase Firestore via REST API over Wi-Fi. Data is stored in sensors/latest and sensor_history collections.

3

Monitor

The web dashboard subscribes to Firestore real-time listeners, updating charts and sensor cards automatically. Control relay states are written back to Firestore instantly.

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

What exactly is ALHYDRA?

ALHYDRA (Algae-Hydroponic Dual-Renewable Apparatus) is a hybrid multi-tower cultivation system that integrates microalgae, horticulture, and hydroponic towers — fully powered by solar and wind energy and monitored in real time through an AIoT cloud platform.

Do I need special hardware to use the dashboard?

The web dashboard works on its own with built-in Demo Mode. To stream live data, connect an ESP32/Arduino with the supported sensors and POST readings to Firebase Firestore via the REST API — full instructions are in Settings → IoT Integration.

What can the AI features do?

You get an on-device machine-learning health score, next-reading forecasting, anomaly detection, and explainable-AI feature attribution — plus an AI assistant that answers questions about your live sensors, energy, and algae cultures.

Is it really powered only by renewables?

Yes — solar panels and a small wind turbine supply the system, with an automatic backup mode and battery state-of-charge tracking. The Environmental Impact page estimates CO₂ avoided, carbon captured, and water saved.

Is it free to use?

The dashboard is open and runs on Firebase Hosting. Just launch it, sign in, and start monitoring — no installation required.
